## Mindfulness Intervention Theory of Change (Longitudinal Design)

Designed a 12-year evaluation framework for a daily mindfulness intervention targeting middle-school students.

**Problem**  
Can a brief, structured mindfulness practice produce measurable long-term improvements in well-being?

**Program Structure**  
- 10-minute daily app-based meditation delivered post-lunch  
- Intervention and comparison schools within the same district  
- Longitudinal measurement from 6th grade through 5 years post–high school  

**Outcome Measure**  
Comprehensive Inventory of Thriving (CIT), a multi-dimensional well-being instrument.

**Evaluation Design**  
- Difference-in-differences (DiD) model  
- Parallel trends validation  
- Explicit internal validity threat analysis

## PHQ-2 / PHQ-9 Screening Workflow Assessment

Human factors and workflow analysis of a depression screening and follow-up process in primary care.

**Problem**  
Identify reliability breakdown points in screening completion and follow-up adherence.

**Approach**  
- Workflow mapping  
- Hierarchical Task Analysis (HTA)  
- Structured evaluation plan for usability and process risk
